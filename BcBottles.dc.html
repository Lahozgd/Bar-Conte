The Story

Before becoming a cocktail, Negroni is a surname, that of Count Camillo Negroni. While he was a Count, he was also an adventurer — a globetrotter, horseman and fencer, an inveterate gambler and a heavy drinker.

Having visited London between 1919 and 1920, he fell in love with gin. Upon his return, he visited his favourite bar, Café Casoni, to seek out drinks using gin.

He asked the barman, Fosco Scarselli, to strengthen his go-to cocktail, the Americano, with "a sprinkle of gin." The barman garnished it with orange instead of lemon, and the iconic Negroni was born.
